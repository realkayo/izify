# Gaara theme art

Generated with built-in ImageGen. Head optimized to a 128 px PNG with alpha;
wallpaper optimized to 1600 × 900 JPEG (quality 88) for offline packaging.

The following vector assets were authored separately in code, not generated by
ImageGen. `seal.svg` outlines the actual 愛 glyph (Yu Gothic) and adds a geometric
stamp frame; it needs no font at runtime. `gourd.svg` is a small clay/sand gourd
with red ties. `liked-songs.svg` composes the seal with desert contours and dunes.
Together they occupy approximately 11 KB; all are embedded for offline use.

Art direction: charcoal/sandstone surfaces, restrained corners, copper-red seal,
gold highlights and an optional cool teal night palette. Desert contours are static.
The head and wallpaper above retain their original illustration and alpha/encoding.

References for the character identity:
[official Gaara birthday article — 愛](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1688),
[official gourd reference](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_2200).
